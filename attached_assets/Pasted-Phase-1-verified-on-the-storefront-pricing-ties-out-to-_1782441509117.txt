What to build:

Minimum-quantity rule: mirror the existing striped-fabric min-qty logic and extend it. Everything admin-editable, including pricing: the wall add-ons (names, images, enabled state, per-grade pricing), the stem price, and the min-quantity threshold and note text.

Display rule: no thousands separators in any price (no commas), anywhere this work touches price display.
