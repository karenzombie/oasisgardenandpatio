# Frankford — Umbrella Collections and Compatible Bases

This document maps each Frankford umbrella collection to its compatible bases currently available on the Oasis site. Use this as the source of truth for building the Compatible Recommendations feature.

**Key notes before building:**
- Direct mount options (DP, IG, BZ2-SM, NGU-DP, DP-ST, IG-ST, IG-GIANT) are permanent installation choices, not portable bases. Include them in the list but do not mark them as Recommended — let the customer self-select if they need a deck mount or in-ground option.
- Table-use-only bases (50C, 23, 50S, 20G-SQ, 24G) are excluded from all recommendations. They are not suitable for free-standing use.
- Stems (8ST, 18ST, 18ST2, MLA-8ST2) and wheel kits (HDW-Kit) are accessories required with certain bases. Per the agreed plan, handle those as a separate phase when base product pages get their own Compatible Recommendations section.
- Beach and Lifeguard umbrellas use sand anchors, not bases. They are excluded from this list.
